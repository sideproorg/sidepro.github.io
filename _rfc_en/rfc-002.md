**Open: it is the first decision still missing.** Some companies forbid their employees from contributing to code under certain licenses (AGPL, above all), and some projects need nobody to close what was made for them. The board proposed a two-part regime: what sidepro designs, under open licenses; each project's data and knowledge, under its own protocols. What remains to decide is the default rule for a request's code: whether the project chooses from a short list of open licenses, what that list is, and whether AGPL is on it.

If your contract forbids some license, your answer is worth more than any opinion: comment on this RFC's PR or write to the project email. While it is open, the FAQ says "we don't know yet", and anyone with a restrictive contract can wait for it to close before signing up.
