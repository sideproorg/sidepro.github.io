Two things make you a fessional

  1. Working in IT today for a company or organization, as an employee or on a steady contract. It is declared, not verified, and the employer's name is never asked for or published.
  2. Bringing a socio-environmental organization from where you live. Then you sign up for that request or any other in /jects. Or, instead of bringing one, signing up for two that are already there.

A club, a soup kitchen, a cooperative, a market, a community library, a neighborhood center, a school. They almost always keep something in a notebook or a WhatsApp group where it gets lost.
