/fessionals

Who is part of it, what they are on, and what they left running.

One card per person goes here, only if they wanted to appear and with what they wanted to show: name or alias, city, what they know how to do, the request they brought, the one they are working on and, if they asked, the repo with their name. Never the employer, never a public email. Operators on the other side go here too, if they want and with their project's yes.

Every request works in an open repo. You can read how another pair solved a form, a backup or a hard decision, commit by commit, with the why written next to it. It is a way to watch other professionals work up close, and to learn from it without asking anyone's permission.

Two things make you a fessional

  1. Working in IT today for a company or organization, as an employee or on a steady contract. It is declared, not verified, and the employer's name is never asked for or published. It is what makes this a side project and not unpaid work.
  2. Bringing a socio-environmental organization from your city or area that already knows what it needs, with someone who will operate it and is willing to sign the one-page request. It has to exist already and not be yours: what's missing is for them to say. Then you sign up for that request or any other in /jects. Or, instead, signing up for two requests already in /jects.

A club, a soup kitchen, a cooperative, a market, a community library, a neighborhood center, a school. They almost always keep something in a notebook or a WhatsApp group where it gets lost.
