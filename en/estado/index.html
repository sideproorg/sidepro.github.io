Current state

What exists and what doesn't, today.

This block is in the footer of every page and is updated every time something changes. If anything here sounds bigger than it is, that's a mistake and it gets fixed. If it's more than thirty days old, anyone can update it with a PR, even just to say "no news".

How it is updated

  1. Edit _data/estado.yml and _data/en/estado.yml in the repo (the browser is enough).
  2. Change the date and only what changed. No counters, no adjectives.
  3. Open a PR. With one approval it goes up on its own.
